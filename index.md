# Privacy Policy — Personal WHOOP Integration

**Last updated:** November 11, 2026

This application is a personal integration intended to allow its owner to access and analyze their WHOOP fitness and wellness data using AI-assisted tools.

**Data accessed:** With the account owner's authorization, the integration may access WHOOP sleep, recovery, heart rate, HRV, strain, workouts, calorie expenditure, and body measurements.

**Purpose:** Data is used to support personal fitness tracking, nutrition planning, training analysis, and recovery assessment.

**Data storage and security:** Access tokens and any stored data will be handled through the selected integration infrastructure. Access will be restricted to the authorized account owner and necessary service providers.

**Third-party processing:** Authorized WHOOP information may be shared with the AI service used for analysis, subject to that service's applicable terms and privacy practices.

**Data sharing:** Personal health data will not be sold or used for advertising.

**Data access and deletion:** The account owner can revoke access through WHOOP's authorization settings. Any data retained by the integration can be deleted upon request.
